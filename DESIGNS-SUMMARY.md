## 🎮 DESIGN 06: GAMING ANIME RPG

**Aesthetic:** Retro 8-bit meets modern anime
**Features:**
- ⚔️ RPG quest system (Shop, Inventory, Achievements, Quest tabs)
- 📊 Real-time stats (Level, EXP, Coins)
- 💥 Rarity system (LEGENDARY, MYTHIC, GODLY, ULTIMATE, EPIC, SUPREME)
- 📈 Power stats (ATK, DEF, SPD for each card)
- 🎮 Game-like interactions (buttons with scales/transforms)
- 🏆 Achievement badges
- 💰 Coin economy system
- 📦 Inventory management

**Color Scheme:** Blue-purple gradient background, yellow/gold accents
**Typography:** Arial Black, bold, arcade-style
**Animations:** Bounce effects, pulse animations, scale transforms

**Best for:** Gaming communities, anime enthusiasts, kids/teens, Pokemon/Yu-Gi-Oh fans
